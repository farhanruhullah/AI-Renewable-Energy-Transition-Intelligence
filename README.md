# 🌍 AI Renewable Energy Transition Intelligence Platform

### AI-powered energy analytics, carbon prediction, and decision-support system using Machine Learning, Explainable AI, and Energy Transition Assessment


# 📌 Project Overview

The **AI Renewable Energy Transition Intelligence Platform** is an interactive AI-based decision-support system designed to analyze global and country-level energy transition progress.

The platform combines:

- Machine Learning for CO₂ emissions prediction
- Explainable AI (SHAP) for model interpretation
- Energy Transition Readiness Index (ETRI) for transition assessment
- Scenario simulation for future renewable pathways
- Dynamic policy support for evidence-based recommendations


The goal is to transform complex energy data into interpretable insights that support understanding of carbon reduction opportunities and renewable energy transitions.


---

# 🎯 Key Features


## 🌍 Energy Intelligence Dashboard

- Global and country-level energy analysis
- Renewable energy indicators
- Fossil dependency assessment
- CO₂ emissions per capita trends
- Electricity generation mix visualization


## 🤖 AI Carbon Prediction

- Predicts CO₂ emissions per capita
- Uses energy, socioeconomic, and transition indicators
- Allows scenario-based variable adjustment


## 🔍 Explainable AI (SHAP)

Provides transparency by identifying:

- Positive CO₂ contribution factors
- Negative CO₂ contribution factors
- Important energy transition drivers


## 📊 Energy Transition Readiness Index (ETRI)

Evaluates transition readiness using:

- Renewable Adoption
- Fossil Reduction
- Clean Infrastructure
- Transition Momentum


## 🔮 Scenario Simulator

Allows exploration of future pathways:

- Renewable electricity expansion
- Fossil reduction scenarios
- Estimated CO₂ impact


## 🏛 Dynamic Policy Decision Support

Generates evidence-based insights using:

- ETRI weaknesses
- SHAP influential factors
- Energy indicators
